# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Launch

- Early game adventurer phase: character creation, shared tavern, reputation loop, guild founding
- Core guild management and recruitment loop
- 6 dungeons across 3 tiers
- 12 traits at launch; 4 member classes
- Idle and Active run modes — determined by player character presence
- Basic guild hall with 4 facilities

## Phase 2 — Post-Launch (Month 3)

- Guild vs. Guild competitive events
- Seasonal dungeon: limited-time themed encounters
- 10 additional traits including class-specific variants
- Member relationship system: friendships and rivalries between specific members
- **Run continuation on player character death** — choice to send remaining party on at reduced odds, converting the active run to idle mid-run. See [[early-game#Player Character Death]].

## Phase 3 — Expansion (Month 6)

- Alliance system: connect your guild with other player guilds for raid events
- Prestige mechanic: reset guild for massive permanent bonuses
- New class: Bard (Support with morale and trait manipulation abilities)
- Cross-platform leaderboards and community dungeon challenges
- **Unclaimed loot reserve** — items not assigned after a run flow into a stash reserve for later use or disenchanting. See [[systems/loot#Loot Distribution]].
- **Additional reputation sources** — NPC contracts, world events, rival guild encounters. See [[systems/reputation]].
